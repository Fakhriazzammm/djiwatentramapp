# Receipt details end to end

One receipt becomes one expense; its itemized document is retained independently of category. Camera and upload share the same review. Quantity (including weight), unit price, item discount/net line amount, receipt discount, tax/service/shipping/other/rounding, included taxes, tender and change are distinct. Unknown amounts remain unknown and discrepancies block confirmation. No fabricated balancing fees.

The API stores original OCR and reviewed document separately. Confirmation locks the receipt and atomically saves the reviewed document and one deterministic ledger entry; retry cannot double-charge. Details are fetched by receipt ID with the authenticated user, including on other devices. Legacy receipts show the existing item list and tax without inventing missing unit prices or changing their ledger.
